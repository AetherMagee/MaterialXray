Material Xray is an Android proxy client built on [Xray-core](https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core), with a Material Design 3 interface. It manages your subscriptions, servers, routing and per-app rules, and runs the tunnel in one of two modes: as a standard Android VPN, or through root-managed routing that keeps the tunnel out of other apps' sight.

:::note
Material Xray is a client, not a service. You need a proxy server or a subscription of your own.
:::

## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Subscriptions" icon="list-format">
		Servers grouped by subscription, with traffic quota, expiry, announcements and latency tests.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Protocols" icon="puzzle">
		VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, WireGuard, HTTP, SOCKS and raw Xray JSON.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Per-app proxy" icon="setting">
		Choose which apps use the proxy. In root mode, assign apps to specific servers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Routing" icon="random">
		Custom rules on domains, IPs, ports and protocols, or rules supplied by your provider.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
